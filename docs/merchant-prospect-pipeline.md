## Pilot success measures

- Count owner replies and confirmed business relationships separately from page views.
- For each consented venue, record only owner-approved public details and the date checked.
- Track whether a venue owner corrects or confirms data, and whether anglers use directions/contact links where analytics permits.
- Charge nothing in the first pilot. Consider a paid offer only after real, attributable customer enquiries and explicit operator interest are demonstrated.
